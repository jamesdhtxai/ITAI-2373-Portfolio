# Applied AI Across Industries — Research, Ethics & Strategy Portfolio

**James Delgado**

This portfolio documents my study of how artificial intelligence is applied across **finance, manufacturing, government, retail, entertainment, and other real-world domains**. The emphasis is not only on what AI can do, but also on how to evaluate its usefulness, risks, ethics, and organizational impact.

## What this portfolio demonstrates

Across 12 modules, I practiced translating AI concepts into practical questions:

- What problem is AI being used to solve?
- What data or decision process does the system depend on?
- What benefits does automation or prediction create?
- What ethical, social, or operational risks should be considered?
- How should an organization evaluate whether an AI use case is appropriate?

## Skills developed

- AI use-case and case-study analysis
- Research synthesis
- Technical and executive communication
- Ethical and risk analysis
- Comparing AI applications across industries
- Connecting AI capabilities to business and operational problems
- Evaluating benefits, limitations, and unintended consequences

## Why this work matters

This coursework strengthened my ability to look beyond the model itself and think about **where AI fits inside a real organization**. I learned to evaluate AI from technical, operational, ethical, and decision-making perspectives rather than treating every use case as a purely technical problem.

That systems-level view complements my hands-on learning in machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, and AI tooling.
